Historical Statistical Evidence Analyzer

Changed files:
- backend/market_lab/historical_analysis.py
- backend/market_lab/runtime.py
- tests/test_historical_analysis.py

New command:
python -m market_lab.runtime historical-analyze --input <evidence.csv> --output <analysis.json>

Design:
- observational/descriptive only; no strategy signal generation
- uses dataset-derived 20/40/60/80 percentile buckets
- reports row counts and distinct-session counts
- reports per-horizon mean/median/mean absolute move/stdev/directional percentages
- reports per-session mean direction percentages to avoid treating minute rows as independent
- reports Pearson feature/forward correlations
- derives OI imbalance as Put OI change % minus Call OI change %
- preserves missing values; no interpolation or synthetic zeros
