PCR Trade Quality Classifier V1
================================

Adds:
- backend/market_lab/pcr_trade_quality_classifier_v1.py
- backend/market_lab/pcr_trade_quality_score_v1.py
- tests/test_pcr_trade_quality_classifier_v1.py
- tests/test_pcr_trade_quality_score_v1.py

Research design:
- Development-only training: TRAIN + OOS_A + OOS_B + OOS_C + OOS_D.
- OOS_E/F/G/H are rejected as training inputs.
- Separate transparent logistic models for BEARISH and BULLISH.
- Features are restricted to values available at or before entry.
- forward_change_* columns are explicitly prohibited from feature use.
- Label is realized frozen +5/-10/15m policy after 0.50 percentage-point cost.
- Direction-specific decision threshold is calibrated using leave-one-development-block-out predictions only.
- Final model is frozen before untouched OOS-H.
- Scoring never retrains or recalibrates on the block being scored.

Important:
This patch does not claim a profitable classifier. It creates the leakage-safe
research/training/scoring pipeline. E/F/G may be scored retrospectively only as
diagnostics. Untouched OOS-H is the first clean classifier validation after the
model spec is frozen.
