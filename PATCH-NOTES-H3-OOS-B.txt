H3 Robustness Expansion patch

Changed/new files:
- backend/market_lab/historical_oos_combined.py
- backend/market_lab/runtime.py
- tests/test_historical_oos_combined.py
- examples/historical-oos-b-20-manifest.json

New command:
python -m market_lab.runtime historical-validate-h3-combined-oos \
  --block-a data/historical-evidence/evidence-oos.csv \
  --block-b data/historical-evidence/evidence-oos-b.csv \
  --spec data/historical-evidence/h3-nx-spec.json \
  --output data/historical-evidence/h3-oos-combined-validation.json

Frozen H3 threshold is never recalculated.
Combined robustness gates:
- both OOS blocks have positive 15m mean directional points
- combined 15m win rate >= 52%
- combined 15m median > 0
- combined 15m mean > 0
- combined event-session coverage >= 12 sessions
- combined mean MFE > mean MAE

OOS-B manifest intentionally excludes 2026-06-26 because it is an NSE F&O holiday (Muharram).
