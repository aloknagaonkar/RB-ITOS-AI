PCR Positioning Event Join v1

- Research-only join of frozen bullish/bearish Stage-2 first-entry events to positioning sidecar.
- Scans exact timestamps T0 through T0+5m.
- Bearish confirmation = any Moving ATM +/-5 strike with combined_5m STRONG_BEARISH.
- Bullish confirmation = any Moving ATM +/-5 strike with combined_5m STRONG_BULLISH.
- Reports first confirmation offset and confirmation rate by existing reversal outcome label.
- No interpolation, no nearest timestamp lookup, no trade signal and no option-P&L assumption.
