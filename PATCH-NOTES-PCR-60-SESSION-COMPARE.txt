PCR 60-Session Comparison v1

Adds:
- backend/market_lab/pcr_deep_compare.py
- runtime command historical-pcr-deep-compare
- tests/test_pcr_deep_compare.py

Purpose:
Compare frozen PCR deep-analysis candidates across TRAIN, OOS-A and OOS-B without retuning thresholds.

Frozen candidates compared:
1. moving PCR < 0.70 AND 5m rising -> expected BULLISH
2. moving PCR > 1.25 AND 5m falling -> expected BEARISH
3. moving 5m+15m bearish while 30m rising -> expected BEARISH early-turn candidate

Primary comparison horizon: +15m.
CONSISTENT requires expected-sign mean and median in all three blocks with non-empty samples.
MIXED requires expected direction in two of three blocks.
No BUY/SELL signal is emitted.
